How to play

Launch each piece into the 8×8 grid from any of its four edges. A piece slides in until it hits something, so pick the side that lands it where you want. Fill a row or column to clear it, and chain clears for bigger scores.

Common questions

My piece stopped short of the gap I wanted.

Pieces stop at the first tile in their path. Try launching from a different edge to reach gaps behind other tiles.

How does Daily Edgefall work?

There's one new board each day, the same for every player. Play it every day to build your streak; missing a whole day resets it.

The tips stopped showing. Can I see them again?

Yes. Open How to play or Settings from the home screen and tap Show tips again.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the gear on the home screen, or pause a game, and switch off Music and sound or Haptics.

My scores or achievements aren't showing in Game Center.

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app and connected to the internet. Edgefall saves your progress and sends it the next time Game Center is available.

Does Edgefall have ads or in-app purchases?

Edgefall is free with ads on the home screen and between runs, never during play. One optional purchase, Remove Ads, turns them off for good. Every mode and level is included either way.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Privacy

Edgefall doesn't collect personal data itself; ads are served by Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.
