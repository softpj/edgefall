Edgefall Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

Edgefall doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your scores, Trial medals, Daily Edgefall streak and settings are saved only on your device. They're removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Edgefall sends your Endless best score and the achievements you earn to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and your profile. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your Game Center details. You can play without Game Center.

Children

Edgefall is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Email softpj@icloud.com.
