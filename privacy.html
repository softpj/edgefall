Edgefall Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

Edgefall is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. We don't collect, store or sell any personal data ourselves, and there's no account to create. Buying Remove Ads turns every ad off.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in Edgefall may collect information such as your IP address, a device or advertising identifier provided by iOS, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, so ads can be delivered, measured and protected against fraud. Google handles this data under Google's policy for partner apps.

Edgefall does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Ads only appear on the home screen and between runs, never while you're playing.

Remove Ads purchase

The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Your purchase is linked to your Apple Account so you can restore it on your other devices.

What stays on your device

Your scores, Trial medals, Daily Edgefall streak and settings are saved only on your device. They're removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Edgefall sends your Endless best score and the achievements you earn to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and your profile. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your Game Center details. You can play without Game Center.

Children

Edgefall is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Email softpj@icloud.com.
